8. rosbag運用

use_sim_time=true + rosbag --clock

Localization

source /opt/ros/humble/setup.bash
source ~/livox_ws/install/setup.bash
source ~/ros2_ws/install/setup.bash

ros2 launch lidar_localization_ros2   mid360_handheld_localization.launch.py   use_sim_time:=true

Bag

ros2 bag play ~/workspace/ros_bag/atr_out   --topics /livox/lidar /livox/imu   --clock   --start-paused

RVizで2D Pose Estimateを与えた後、bag Terminalで SPACE を押して再生します。
